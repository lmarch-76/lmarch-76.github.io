## About me

I am a PhD student at the University of Cambridge supervised by <a href="https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~pll28/">Po-Ling Loh</a>. I finished Part III in mathematics at the University of Cambridge in 2024, focusing on statistics. Before that, I did my undergraduate degree in mathematics at Imperial College London. I am originally from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oradea">Oradea</a>, Romania. My research interests are broadly in algorithmic stability and optimization. Specifically, I work on problems related to differential privacy and robustness.
