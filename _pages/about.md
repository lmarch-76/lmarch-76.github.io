## About me

I am a PhD student at the University of Cambridge, supervised by <a href="https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~pll28/">Po-Ling Loh</a>. In 2024, I finished <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_III_of_the_Mathematical_Tripos">Part III</a> in mathematics at Cambridge, focusing on statistical learning theory and applications. Before that, from 2020 to 2023, I completed my undergraduate degree in mathematics at <a href="https://www.imperial.ac.uk/mathematics/">Imperial College London</a>. I am originally from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oradea">Oradea</a>, Romania. [Here](/images/Deal_C_O.jpg) is a view of Oradea from Ciuperca Hill (“Mushroom Hill”).

I work at the intersection of statistics and computer science. My research focuses on algorithmic stability and its connections to optimization, network inference, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in <a href="https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/privacybook.html">differential privacy</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robust_statistics">robust statistics</a>, including robustness to adversarial contamination and heavy-tailed data. Specifically, I develop broadly applicable, polynomial-time algorithms and estimation methods with provable stability and utility guarantees.
